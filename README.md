# Hi, I'm Atri! 👋

I'm a Data Scientist with an MSc in Astrophysics and a B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering. I work at the intersection of quantitative analysis, intensive research and practical machine learning.

My path into Data Science is unconventional. I've processed gravitational wave signals, classified molecular gas structures in the Milky Way using clustering algorithms, and spent two years in fast-paced operational environments before fully committing to ML and analytics. This background gave me a unique way of thinking about data problems — I am comfortable with messy, high-dimensional, real-world data and I know how to communicate findings to both technical and non-technical audiences.

**Currently open to:** Data Scientist · Data Analyst · ML Engineer roles
